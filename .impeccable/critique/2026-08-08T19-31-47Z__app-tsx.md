## Priority Issues

### [P1] Expert settings collapse several different jobs into one wall

**Why it matters:** Route choice, credentials, model selection, discovery, speech configuration, and launch help are different tasks. Presenting 40 controls and 31 enabled buttons at once makes setup error-prone and support-heavy, particularly on mobile. Immediate persistence of endpoint/key changes further obscures whether a configuration is committed.

**Fix:** Start with outcome-based route templates such as “Private on this device,” “Gemini cloud,” and “Custom endpoints.” Split advanced controls into collapsible Language model, Speech recognition, Credentials, and Launch help sections. Replace chip walls with searchable comboboxes, and add reset/default plus “stored locally” feedback.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Route readiness is visible but not understandable

**Why it matters:** “Route not tested” sits beside the core action yet does not say whether testing is optional, recommended for large files, or required after configuration changes. First-timers may either start nervously or detour into expert settings.

**Fix:** Encode the policy in the status: “Not tested · optional,” “Recommended before a large upload,” or automatically preflight after file selection. Mark one safe default route as recommended and explain failures without requiring users to understand endpoint protocols.

**Suggested commands:** `$impeccable clarify`, `$impeccable onboard`

### [P2] The primary workflow is a card stack, not one staged task

**Why it matters:** Upload, route review, speech hints, and transcript occupy neighboring cards. After selection, the primary action appears in the route card rather than within a clear, numbered sequence. The current focus movement works, but the spatial model still asks users to infer the workflow.

**Fix:** Recompose the surface as **1. Select media → 2. Review route and optional hints → 3. Transcribe**. Compact completed stages, keep the route/privacy sentence adjacent to the action, and demote the empty transcript panel until processing starts.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] Product character and the completion peak are too generic

**Why it matters:** The slate/indigo Tailwind vocabulary says “AI developer tool,” not “trusted transcription workspace.” Completion is functionally complete but lacks a distinctive summary or visual sense of transformation.

**Fix:** Build the identity around transcript craft: editorial typography, restrained waveform/timecode details, a true route diagram, and clearer source-to-text relationships. At completion, foreground word/character count, route, and export readiness before secondary metadata.

**Suggested commands:** `$impeccable bolder`, `$impeccable typeset`
